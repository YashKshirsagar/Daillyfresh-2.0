Refund Policy

Last updated: 15-09-2025

At Daillyfresh, we are committed to delivering premium-quality, fresh dairy products. This Refund Policy outlines the circumstances under which refunds or replacements are issued for any order placed through our WhatsApp, phone, or website channels.

1. Eligibility for Refunds/Replacements

Refunds or replacements will be issued for the following conditions:

  • Spoiled or Expired Products: Milk, eggs, or dairy items that are visibly spoiled, curdled, or past "best before" date upon delivery.
  • Damaged Packaging: Products arrived in damaged or unsealed containers that compromise food safety.
  • Missing Items: Orders delivered incomplete (missing items from the subscription order).
  • Incorrect Items: Wrong product(s) delivered instead of what was ordered.
  • Quality Issues: Products not meeting the quality standard described at the time of order (e.g., sour milk when fresh milk was ordered).

2. Timeframe for Reporting Issues

  • Issues must be reported within 2 hours of delivery.
  • Upon reporting, you must provide photographic or video evidence of the issue along with your order details (order ID, delivery date, items affected).
  • Claims reported after the 2-hour window may not be processed unless there are exceptional circumstances.

3. Refund/Replacement Options

Upon verification of a valid claim, you will be offered one of the following at Daillyfresh's discretion:

  • Replacement: The affected item(s) will be re-delivered in the next scheduled delivery at no additional charge.
  • Credit/Store Credit: The equivalent amount will be credited to your account, usable for future orders.
  • Cash Refund: For cash-on-delivery customers, a refund will be issued in the next delivery or via agreed payment mode.
  • UPI/Bank Transfer Refund: Refunds for digital payments (UPI, bank transfer) will be processed within 5-7 business days.

4. Non-Refundable Items & Scenarios

Refunds will NOT be issued for:

  • Storage Issues: Products spoiled due to improper storage (e.g., left outside, not refrigerated).
  • Usage Beyond Shelf Life: Products consumed past the "best before" or best-by date.
  • Allergic Reactions: Personal allergic reactions (our products are safe per FSSAI and food safety standards).
  • Taste Preferences: Refunds due to personal taste preferences or subjective quality opinions without evidence.
  • Late Reporting: Claims filed more than 2 hours after delivery without exceptional justification.
  • Subscription Cancellation: Cancellations do not guarantee immediate refund of the full advance payment; remaining credit will be processed per subscription terms.

5. Subscription Cancellations & Refunds

  • Subscriptions can be paused or cancelled anytime by contacting us at least 48 hours before the next scheduled delivery.
  • If you cancel mid-subscription, remaining credit (if any) will be refunded or credited per your subscription agreement.
  • For monthly subscriptions, cancellations initiated within the same billing cycle will not be eligible for a full refund; only the unused portion of future cycles will be credited.

6. How to Report & Claim a Refund

To initiate a refund or replacement claim:

  • Contact us within 2 hours of receiving your order via WhatsApp: 8149415113 or Phone: +91-8149415113.
  • Provide your order details (order ID, delivery date, items affected).
  • Share photographic or video evidence of the issue (damaged packaging, spoiled product, missing items, etc.).
  • Our team will review your claim and respond within 24 hours with a resolution.

7. Processing Time for Refunds

  • Replacement Delivery: 1-2 business days (included in next regular delivery).
  • Store Credit/Account Credit: Instant (available immediately for next order).
  • Cash Refund (COD): 1-2 business days (issued during next delivery or via agreed method).
  • UPI/Bank Transfer Refund: 5-7 business days after approval.

8. Quality Assurance & Food Safety

  • All our products comply with FSSAI (Food Safety and Standards Authority of India) regulations and are sourced from verified, trusted farms.
  • Products are delivered in hygienic, temperature-controlled conditions with labelling that includes batch numbers, "best before" dates, and safety certifications.
  • If you suspect a food safety issue (e.g., contamination, unusual taste/odour), contact us immediately and do not consume the product. We will investigate and provide resolution accordingly.

9. Limitation of Liability

  • Daillyfresh's total liability for any refund claim is limited to the cost of the affected delivery or subscription cycle.
  • We are not responsible for indirect, incidental, or consequential losses arising from product issues (e.g., medical expenses, meals affected).

10. Dispute Resolution

  • In case of disputes regarding refund eligibility, both parties agree to resolve the matter through mutual discussion.
  • If unresolved, the matter will be subject to applicable Indian consumer protection laws and the jurisdiction of Nagpur, Maharashtra courts.

11. Policy Updates

  • This Refund Policy may be updated at any time. Changes will be communicated via email or displayed on our website with an updated "Last updated" date.
  • Continued use of our service after policy changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated terms.
